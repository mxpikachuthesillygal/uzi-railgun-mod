# Uzi's SICK AS HELL Railgun
[Link To Steam Workshop Page](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3485586841>)

Bring Uzi Doorman's questionably constructed weapon into your toolset for unprecedented levels of destruction and mobility.

This Magnetically Amplified Photon Converger will vaporize any target in its path. Cars, buildings, helicopters, tanks—it doesn't matter. Just keep that crosshair steady and try to land on your feet.

**Based off the "ORIS RAILGUN" mod by Dzage and Caped Karnage**\

## Known Bugs
- **Status Indicator slides around**
  - This is the result of a game engine limitation and can't be fixed by me.
- **Muzzle particle ghosting effect while firing and in motion**
  - This is also the aforementioned limitation.
- **Third Person**
  - Player must enter first person to scope in.
- **Lag when firing at certain objects/structures**
  - Not 100% certain on the cause, but I think it has to do with indestructible entities.
  - Best example is the Dam on the Lee Chemicals map.
- **Clicking down on the right thumbstick does not enable d-pad inputs on controller**
  - Appears to be caused by something on Tuxedo Labs' end with their default Steam Input layout.
- **Status Indicator briefly disappears**
  - Related to switching between firing modes.
