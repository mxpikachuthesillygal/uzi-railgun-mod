# Uzi's SICK AS HELL Railgun

[Link To Steam Workshop Page](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3485586841>)

Bring Uzi Doorman's questionably constructed weapon into your toolset for unprecedented levels of destruction and mobility.

This Magnetically Amplified Photon Converger will vaporize any target in its path. Cars, buildings, helicopters, tanks—it doesn't matter. Just keep that crosshair steady and try to land on your feet.

**Based off the "ORIS RAILGUN" mod by Dzage and Caped Karnage**\
